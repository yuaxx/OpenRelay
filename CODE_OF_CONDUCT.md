# Contributor Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of OpenRelay, we commit to fostering an open, welcoming, and productive collaboration environment.

## Our Standards

- **Constructive Collaboration**: Treat contributors, reviewers, and users with professional respect. Focus on code quality, architecture, and practical tradeoffs.
- **Safety and Privacy**: Never post, share, or demand live credentials, API keys, session tokens, or personal identifiers in issues, PRs, or public discussions.
- **Care for Upstream and Attribution**: Respect the work of OpenCodex and third-party projects. Retain licenses, attribution, and notices faithfully.
- **Responsibility**: Authors own their contributions, test coverage, and fixes. Disagree with technical reasoning and evidence rather than personal criticism.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and related communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers will review and take appropriate and fair corrective action.
